# Homepage (and site world): Utility Locate

Scope: whole personal site, homepage first; work.html, about-me.html, 404.html inherit. Visitor mode: Persuade.
Audience: hiring managers and recruiters for frontend, fullstack and DevOps roles; laptop between applications or phone from LinkedIn; 30-second skim.
Action: open Work & Projects. Proof: component library + containers (10 microfrontends, 15–20 engineers, WCAG 2.1 AA); Podkrepi payments (73 merged PRs, public GitHub search); homelab backups that restore-test (26 services). Availability: Jan 2027, EU, open to relocation.
Constraints: Atkinson Hyperlegible Next + Mono; heading morph (view transitions) kept; existing copy, URLs, cv.pdf, PostHog; static HTML/CSS on Cloudflare; WCAG 2.1 AA.
Memorable moment: three sprayed locate lines crossing the first viewport, each flagged with its proof.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The site is a street surface marked for what is buried beneath it: the work of building the layer underneath is shown as utility locate marks. It refuses the dark dev portfolio with a neon accent and its card row.
OWN-WORLD: Asphalt ground, poured-concrete kerb strip for reading surfaces, locate paint in the real colour code used as information: orange = frontend (communications), blue = fullstack (water), red = operations (power), white = proposed dig (availability). Paint draws lines, flags and marks; labels stay light text that passes AA. Atkinson Next for words, Atkinson Mono only for locate tags.
STORY: A recruiter sees what Dimitar builds, reads three flagged proofs across the stack, learns the availability from Jan 2027, and opens Work & Projects.
FIRST VIEWPORT: Concrete kerb strip down the left edge. Top strip: name, white availability mark, menu (Work & Projects primary, About, Résumé). Claim at display scale and the summary on asphalt. Three full-width sprayed lines in orange, blue and red, each with a mono locate tag carrying its proof and linking to its Work entry. A working legend at the foot.
