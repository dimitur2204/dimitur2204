# Design System: Dimitar Nizamov

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "Utility Locate"**

The site is a street surface marked for what is buried beneath it. Every page is asphalt with its aggregate showing, bounded on the left by a poured-concrete kerb that is sawcut at intervals and holds still while pages change. The work is shown the way a locate crew marks buried services: sprayed lines in the real utility colour code, each staked with a survey flag and a written tag. Paint carries meaning, never decoration. Words stay light, legible, and pass AA on the ground they sit on.

Density is calm and editorial: a top strip, a large claim, three full-width runs, a working legend. Long reading moves onto a lighter poured-concrete slab with dark ink, the most comfortable contrast on the site. Materials are procedural rasters (asphalt, kerb, slab, spray masks, a dig frame) generated by `website/tools/generate-textures.mjs` with provenance stored beside each file. The few icons are drawn SVG arrows and a flag, tinted through masks.

The world rejects the dark developer portfolio with a single neon accent and a row of cards. There is no accent outside the locate code.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Asphalt ground, concrete kerb on the left edge of every page, concrete slab for long reading.
- Four paint colours with fixed meaning: orange frontend, blue fullstack, red operations, white proposed dig or availability.
- Sprayed runs cross the full width from the kerb to the right edge, each flagged and tagged in mono.
- Atkinson Hyperlegible Next for words; Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono only for locate tags and Work margin data.
- One-time motion: the runs lay down once on load; menu words morph into subpage headings.

## Colors

A dark, warm-grey street with four survey paints that are information, not mood.

### Primary
- **Communications Orange** (#ff6a13): frontend work. Paints the frontend run, flag and key swatch, and doubles as its own text tint for tags, roles and title underlines because it passes AA on asphalt.

### Secondary
- **Water Blue** (#0072ce): fullstack work. Draws lines, flags and key swatches only.
- **Water Blue, Text Tint** (#6cb4f5): every piece of fullstack text and underline; the raw blue is too dark to read on asphalt.

### Tertiary
- **Power Red** (#e4002b): operations work. Draws lines, flags and key swatches only.
- **Power Red, Text Tint** (#ff6b7d): every piece of operations text and underline.
- **Proposed-Dig White** (#f4f3ee): a dig not yet made. Outlines the availability mark, paints the study entry's dashed run, the main nav stroke, the 404 frame, and is the interaction colour for focus rings, hover, selection and the skip link.

### Neutral
- **Asphalt** (#26282a): the ground on every page, under the asphalt texture; also the patch behind a tag that sits on a run.
- **Joint Line** (#3e4144): hairline dividers under the top strip, above the footer, beside about-page facts.
- **Strong Joint** (#55595d): resting underline on quiet links and the rule after section labels.
- **Chalk** (#eeede8): primary text and headings on asphalt.
- **Weathered Chalk** (#bdbcb5): lede, body copy in entries, secondary nav items.
- **Faded Chalk** (#9d9c96): meta lines, notes, the key's footnote; the lowest text tone allowed on asphalt.
- **Poured Concrete** (#cbc8c0): the kerb and the reading slab, under their textures.
- **Concrete Ink** (#1f2021): first paragraph, bold and links on the slab.
- **Concrete Grey Ink** (#45474a): running prose on the slab.

### Named Rules
**The Colour Code Rule.** Orange means frontend, blue fullstack, red operations, white a proposed dig or availability. A colour never appears where its meaning does not apply, and there is no yellow and no other accent.

**The Tint Rule.** Raw blue and red draw paint only; text in those colours uses their text tints. Paint draws lines, flags and marks; labels stay light text that passes AA.

**The Not-Colour-Alone Rule.** Every run carries a written tag naming its kind, and the homepage keeps a working legend, so colour is never the only cue.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next (with ui-sans-serif, system-ui)
**Body Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Next
**Label/Mono Font:** Atkinson Hyperlegible Mono (with ui-monospace, Menlo)

**Character:** One legibility-first family in two voices. Next does all the talking at heavy weights and tight tracking; Mono is the crew's handwriting on the paint, uppercase and widely spaced. Both families are binding.

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (800, clamp(2.4rem → 4.1rem), 0.98): the homepage claim, held to about 15ch.
- **Headline** (800, step 4, 0.95, -0.04em): subpage h1. Its metrics match the menu word it morphs from.
- **Title** (700, step 2, 1.18): the proof titles on the homepage runs and Work entry titles (600 there).
- **Lede** (400, step 1, 1.55): summary under a claim or headline, in Weathered Chalk, up to 74ch.
- **Body** (400, step 0, 1.65): running text, measure 64ch.
- **Section label** (700, step 1, -0.01em): Work section names followed by a hairline rule to the edge.
- **Locate tag** (Mono 500, 0.8125rem, 0.08em, uppercase, tabular figures): the kind and proof written on a run, and the kind and dates in Work's margin.

### Named Rules
**The Crew Handwriting Rule.** Mono appears only in locate tags and the Work margin data (kind, dates). Section labels, about-page fact labels, meta lines and everything else are set in Next, even where they share the label class.

**The Heavy Claim Rule.** Headings set tight (-0.025em to -0.04em) and balanced; body text never goes below 400 or below Faded Chalk on asphalt.

## Layout

Every page reserves a fixed kerb on its left edge (4.5rem, narrowing to 0.75rem at 768px and below); content padding starts after it. The homepage runs in a 1240px column as a full-height flex stack: top strip, claim, runs, legend, footer. Work and About sit in a 1080px column.

The top strip is a two-column grid: name, role and the white availability mark on the left, the menu on the right; it collapses to one column at 860px. Each homepage run is a two-column grid (0.95fr title, 1.05fr text) under a paint line that breaks out of the column to span from the kerb to the right viewport edge; the tag sits on the line at the start of the text column. Below 860px the tag lifts just above the line with its flag staked through the paint, and the grid stacks. A max-height 500px query shrinks the claim so the first run reaches the landscape-phone viewport.

Work entries open with the same full-bleed run (section gap 5.5rem, entry gap 3.5rem, 2.5rem on narrow screens). About uses a prose slab beside a 17rem sticky facts rail, stacking at 860px. The 404 centres a single framed dig.

## Elevation & Depth

Depth is physical, not interface. Surfaces are flat at rest; only the two concrete objects cast shadow, as kerb and slab would on a street. Paint lies on the asphalt with no lift.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Kerb cast** (`box-shadow: 0.35rem 0 0.9rem -0.2rem rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): the kerb onto the road, with a lit lip and a shaded face drawn inside its right edge.
- **Slab cast** (`box-shadow: inset 0 1px 0 #f1efe9, 0 0.5rem 1.25rem -0.4rem rgba(0, 0, 0, 0.6)`): the reading slab, with a lit top edge.

### Named Rules
**The Only Concrete Casts Rule.** Shadows belong to concrete objects. Text, links, paint and controls never cast or lift.

## Shapes

Square and material. Concrete edges and the slab are unrounded; paint takes its shape from spray masks with overspray, uneven density and tapered ends, and the dig outline is a sprayed dashed frame. Small softening appears only on drawn strokes and swatches (2px on the nav underline and the about-page dash, 0.2rem on key swatches), on focus rings (3px), and on the skip link (6px). The flag is a staff and a notched pennant.

## Components

### Locate Run (signature)
The device of the world. A sprayed line from the kerb to the right edge, 1.5rem tall, in the paint of its kind; each run uses its own spray mask so no two passes look alike. A survey flag in the same paint is staked into it, and a Mono tag in the text tint names the kind and its proof. On wide screens the tag sits on the line on an asphalt patch; on narrow screens it lifts above the line without the patch. On load the homepage runs lay down once, left to right (0.9s, staggered 0.14s), only when reduced motion is not requested. Proof titles under a run are underlined in the run's tint and take that tint on hover.

### Dig Mark
White sprayed dashed outline around the availability line in the top strip; the same frame becomes the legend swatch and the entire 404 page. Study on Work is a dashed white run, a dig proposed and not yet made.

### Navigation
- **Style:** Next, 600, Weathered Chalk with 0.6rem block padding for target size. The primary item (Work & Projects) is Chalk at step 1, weight 800, permanently marked with a 3px white painted stroke.
- **Hover / Focus:** text goes to Chalk and a white stroke draws from the left (0.35s).
- **Mobile:** the menu drops under the identity and aligns left.
- **Back link:** "Index" with a drawn left arrow that nudges 4px on hover; colour shifts to the dig white.

### Heading Morph (binding)
The menu word you click becomes the heading of the page it opens through named view transitions (0.6s). The old page recedes and blurs; the new one rises. Returning reverses it. Snapshots scale by height and pin left so word and heading line up. The kerb has its own transition name and no animation: it holds still throughout. Reduced motion disables view transitions.

### Colour Key
A working legend at the homepage foot: short rounded swatches for orange, blue and red, a dig-frame swatch for white, each named in plain words, plus a faded note that the colours follow the utility locate code.

### Work Entry
A full-bleed run, then the margin data (flag, kind, dates) in Mono on an asphalt patch, the place in Faded Chalk, a title, the role in the tint, a summary, short tinted dash bullets, a run of tool names separated by middots, and a tinted link with a drawn arrow.

### Cards / Containers
- **Concrete slab:** the only container. Square, Poured Concrete under its texture, 2.5rem padding (1.5rem narrow), Concrete Grey Ink prose at step 1 with line height 1.7, Concrete Ink for the opening paragraph, bold and links. Selection inverts to ink.

### Links
Quiet links rest with a Strong Joint underline and hover to the dig white or Chalk. Focus everywhere is a 2px dig-white outline offset 3px.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the kerb fixed on the left edge of every page and out of every transition.
- **Do** mark every kind of work with its code colour and a written tag; add the legend wherever the code first appears.
- **Do** use the -text tints for any blue or red text; orange may be its own text tint.
- **Do** run paint full width from the kerb to the right edge, using a different spray mask per run.
- **Do** use the dig white for focus, hover and selection, and for availability.
- **Do** put long reading on the concrete slab with ink, not on asphalt.
- **Do** keep motion to the one-time lay-down and the heading morph, both off under reduced motion.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add yellow or any colour outside the locate code, including for hover or emphasis.
- **Don't** use a code colour where its meaning does not apply.
- **Don't** set anything other than locate tags and Work margin data in Mono.
- **Don't** set text in raw blue (#0072ce) or raw red (#e4002b).
- **Don't** wrap work in cards or add glows; shadows belong only to kerb and slab.
- **Don't** use glyph or icon-font arrows; use the drawn SVG set tinted through masks.
- **Don't** replay the lay-down animation on scroll or hover.
