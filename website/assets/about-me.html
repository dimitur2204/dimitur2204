About me

I build the layer other developers build on — component libraries, design systems, and the tooling around them.

I've been writing frontend professionally for five years, most of them at UNIwise in Aarhus, where I own the React and TypeScript component library that every one of our frontends depends on. It means most of my work is judged by people who never see it directly: the engineers who install the package, and the students sitting an exam on the other side of it, who only notice the interface when it fails them.

Accessibility is a large part of why I like that job. When you are the shared dependency, getting WCAG right once means ten applications get it for free — and getting it wrong once means ten applications are broken at the same time. That leverage, in both directions, is the most interesting thing about library work. Right now I'm migrating the whole ecosystem off Semantic UI React onto headless primitives without regressing a single one of those apps.

I grew up in Bulgaria and have been in Denmark for a few years now. Alongside work I'm finishing a BEng in Software Technology Engineering at VIA University College in January 2027. I deliberately picked the coursework my day job doesn't cover — embedded C, databases, backend systems — and my bachelor's project is with the radiology department at Aarhus University Hospital, applying machine learning to medical imaging to relate organ health to biological age.

Outside work there's a Debian server in my apartment running Nextcloud, Plex and Immich behind Tailscale, with Grafana dashboards I check more often than I need to. Self-hosting has taught me more about Docker, networking and backups than any amount of reading would have — mostly by breaking, at inconvenient hours. I also use Neovim, btw.
